# Instance 0.22.9

Owners can manage approvals for all chats with an agent under **Access &
credentials → Approvals**. Ask remains the default. Choose which connected
clients may save an **Always allow** rule, review those rules, and revoke them.
Supporting engines describe the exact matching scope; the connector presents
the choices without keeping a separate approval cache.

An explicit **Automatically approve permitted actions** setting approves current
requests offered by the engine, including access grants. Hard engine restrictions,
operating-system permissions and work limits still apply. Revoking a remembered
rule stops future matches; Automatic continues independently until changed back
to Ask. Already accepted work or conversation grants may continue until completed,
stopped or revoked through the engine.

Decisions are bound to the authenticated connection and current request. Stale
responses, cancelled work, changed connections and conflicting settings cannot
silently create new authority. An uncertain reply is not automatically replayed.
Older engines keep ordinary current-request approvals; reusable rules require
the optional negotiated profile.

Fresh consumer packages bundle the matching Runtime 0.15.10. Existing
installations retain their selected engine, models, accounts and work. The update
does not enable Automatic or silently delegate clients.
